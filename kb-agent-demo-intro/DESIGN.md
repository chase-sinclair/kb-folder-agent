## Style Prompt
Create a dark enterprise-tech motion style for an AI product demo. The scene should feel precise, modern, and premium rather than playful. Use a deep navy canvas with subtle cyan light, a warm manila folder as the focal object, and crisp document cards that bloom outward with controlled motion. The overall feeling should be "organized complexity revealed."

## Colors
- `#06101d` - background base
- `#0c1e33` - panel and grid accents
- `#f0b44c` - folder body
- `#ffd483` - folder highlight
- `#6ee7f5` - cyan accent glow

## Typography
- `Space Grotesk`
- `Inter Tight`

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use generic bright blue gradients.
- Do not make the motion bouncy or cartoonish.
- Do not overcrowd the frame with readable file text.
- Do not add marketing slogans or UI chrome to this intro.
